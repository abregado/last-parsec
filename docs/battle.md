# Battle

`js/rules/battle.js` (`LP.battle`) handles the rules; the header documents `campaign.battle` and the field specs. The tab is `js/ui/battleTab.js`.

- **Fields:** built in order by the `FIELDS` builders (deployment, objective, enemy category, enemy, numbers, Uniques, weapons and more). Each builder can read earlier slots, and returns null when it doesn't apply. Slots use the same random/fixed/forced pattern as characters.
- **Enemy data:** profiles come from `js/rules/enemies.js`. Enemies without models (Models tab, `js/rules/models.js`) are never rolled, and neither are weapons switched off there.
- **Turn link:**
  - The mission and named Rival come from the turn (`FROM_TURN`, the `useTurn` helper in the tab).
  - A named Rival locks the enemy and limits its Uniques. Hated Rivals add +1.
  - An invaded planet locks the invader.
- **Crew:** with a crew, named members are deployed (`setDeployed` / `defaultDeployed`). This sets the crew in the field and Seize the Initiative's highest Savvy and Feral inputs.
- **Results:** `summary()` gives the facts Post-Battle stage 4.0 pre-fills from.
- **Extras:** Seize the Initiative and Battle Events are at the end of `battle.js`.

## Table view

`table.html`, opened by the Table view button on the Battle tab (once a battle is generated). A reference and Out tracker for a phone or tablet in landscape, all on one scrolling screen with large buttons.

- **Layout:** a brief (objective, deployment, sight, terrain, bounty), then the crew, the crew's Consumables (when it has any) and the enemy force. Each unit is one row: name (with species and Class beneath) and stats, with its species, Class and gear rules, on the left, weapons with their trait rules on the right. Rules for a whole team sit above its table. Battle Events can be rolled at the bottom.
- **Tracking:** only Out (the skull cell). Nothing else is tracked or rolled for you.
- **Model:** `js/rules/tableModel.js` (`LP.tableModel.build`) turns the battle and roster into plain JSON; its header documents the shape. The Battle tab passes it to the page in the URL hash (`LP.tableStore.encode` / `decode`), because Firefox gives each `file://` page its own storage. The page never touches campaign data and needs no rules code. It keeps only its own marks (Out, rolled events) in its own localStorage, and they reset when a new battle is generated.
- **Live:** while the window is open, the Battle tab sets a new hash on every change, and the page redraws on `hashchange`.
- **Sharing:** Copy table link (shown only on the hosted `http(s)` copy) puts the full table view URL on the clipboard. The link carries the whole battle, so other players open it on their phones with no campaign data and no server; it is a snapshot, and each device keeps its own Out marks.
- **Gear data:** `js/data/gear.js` (GENERATED by `python tools/build-gear.py` from the local rulebook PDF) holds weapon stats, trait rules and what each item does. Enemy weapons are matched by name; an unknown weapon shows dashes.
- **Styles:** `css/table.css` (standalone, with the same colour tokens as `css/styles.css`).
